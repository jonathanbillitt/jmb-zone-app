JMB Lighting — Safety & Safe Operation Guide

JMB one / 4our / 8ight LED lighting controllers · Read before installation and keep for future reference.

1. Intended use

JMB controllers drive low-voltage constant-voltage LED strip and fixtures (RGB, tuneable white, single-colour and infrared) for film, TV, broadcast, stage and architectural use, controlled by DMX512, CRMX wireless or the companion Bluetooth app. They are professional equipment intended for installation and operation by competent persons.

Indoor, dry locations only. The units are not sealed against water or dust and must not be used outdoors unprotected, in wet or condensing environments, or where flammable vapours are present.

Any other use — including driving non-LED loads, mains loads, or exceeding the ratings below — is outside the intended use. [LEGAL: liability wording for misuse.]

2. Electrical safety

  • JMB 4our and 8ight contain a mains power supply. Connect only to a properly earthed mains outlet of the voltage stated on the rating plate. Do not open the enclosure: no user-serviceable parts inside; hazardous voltages are present when connected. Disconnect from the mains before any cleaning, fuse replacement or cabling work.
  • JMB one is powered from an external 12 V or 24 V DC supply or battery pack. Use a supply rated for the connected load, with the correct polarity; the unit is not protected against reverse supply connection at the input.
  • Output circuits are separated extra-low voltage (24 V DC). Route output wiring away from mains cabling.
  • Use conductor sizes rated for the configured port current (up to 6.5 A per output). Undersized strip feed wiring is a fire risk that no controller protection can fully remove.

3. Fuses

Each output is protected electronically (configurable current limit, microsecond short-circuit trip, over-temperature trip) and by a replaceable cartridge fuse.

  • Replace only with the same type and rating: 5 × 20 mm glass, time-delay, T10A L 250 V (IEC 60127-2).
  • Never fit a higher rating, a fast-blow substitute, or bridge a fuse.
  • A fuse that blows repeatedly indicates a wiring or load fault — find and fix the cause; do not keep replacing the fuse.

4. Load limits and heat

  • Maximum per-output current: 6.5 A (configurable lower; set the limit to suit the connected strip). Total load must remain within the PSU rating.
  • Mount with free airflow. Do not cover the enclosure, stack heat-producing equipment on it, or enclose it without ventilation. Ambient operating range: 0–40 °C. [Confirm final ambient rating before print.]
  • The unit reduces output automatically as fixtures approach their configured temperature limit and shuts a port down on a hard over-temperature; this protects the equipment and is not a substitute for correct installation.

5. Flashing-light effects — photosensitive epilepsy warning

Several built-in effects (Strobe, Lightning, Police, Paparazzi, Explosion, Club and others) and DMX-driven strobe control produce intense flashing light. A small percentage of people may experience seizures when exposed to flashing light, even with no prior history.

  • Warn cast, crew and audience before using strobe effects.
  • Do not aim strobing fixtures at stairways, exits or moving-machinery areas.
  • Stop use immediately if anyone experiences dizziness, disorientation or involuntary movement. [LEGAL: standard PSE warning wording.]

6. Infrared (IR) fixtures — invisible radiation

The controller can drive infrared LED strip and fixtures (Dim personality). IR output is invisible: a fixture can be at full power while appearing dark.

  • Treat any connected IR fixture as ON whenever the system is powered.
  • Do not stare into IR emitters at close range; position high-power IR sources so that prolonged close viewing is not possible. Fixture photobiological classification (IEC 62471) is the fixture manufacturer's responsibility — observe the fixture's own safety group and distances.
  • Remember that camera monitoring shows IR that the eye cannot see — use a camera to confirm an IR rig is actually off before working close to it.

7. Radio equipment

The unit contains Bluetooth® Low Energy and, when fitted, a CRMX™ 2.4 GHz radio. Do not use where radio transmitters are prohibited. The radio link is not a safety-of-life channel: do not build safety functions (e.g. emergency blackout for egress lighting) solely on the wireless path. [Compliance: Radio Equipment Regulations / RED assessment + Bluetooth SIG qualification to be completed before sale.]

8. Installation practice

  • Secure mounting only; do not suspend by cables.
  • Strain-relieve all output wiring; chafed strip wiring is the most common cause of output faults.
  • After installation, verify each port's current limit and temperature trip are set appropriately for the connected fixture, and function-test the fault clear path.
  • The DMX/CRMX input, app and front panel all change live output. Establish who has control before working on fixtures — a port can light at any time while the system is powered. Isolate power before touching wiring.

9. Maintenance, service and modification

No user-serviceable parts inside any unit. Cleaning: dry or slightly damp cloth, mains disconnected, no solvents. Repairs only by JMB Lighting or its authorised agents; unauthorised opening, repair or modification ends the warranty [LEGAL: warranty terms reference] and may render the equipment unsafe.

10. Disposal

Do not dispose of in general waste. The unit is electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE): recycle through an appropriate collection scheme. [Confirm WEEE producer registration + crossed-bin marking before sale.]
