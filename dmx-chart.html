Personalities (per port)

The number is the one the panel, the app and an RDM console all show. The RGBW and RGBWA personalities need a five-output port module (the .3 boards: outputs red, green, blue, white, amber on XLR pins 2–6, printed 1 V+ 2 R 3 G 4 B 5 W 6 A); on a three-output module they are greyed out and cannot be selected.

New in firmware 3.3.0: CW/WW FX, number 5. RGB 8-bit to RGBWA FX moved up one (6–14), on the panel, in the app and on an RDM console; a console that stored a personality number from an earlier firmware should be re-patched.

CW/WW on a five-output module puts cool white on W (pin 5) and warm white on A (pin 6), and leaves red, green and blue dark. Wire a tuneable-white tape's cool lead to W and its warm lead to A. On a three-output module cool rides the green terminal and warm the red one, as it always has.

Dim personalities patch a port like a classic dimmer channel: the one DMX level scales the port's set colour (chosen in the app). With no colour set it acts as white — every output carries the level — so a single-colour or IR fixture lights straight from its one patched channel, whichever output terminal it's wired to.

Each port's whole footprint must fit inside the universe, so the start address caps at 513 − footprint: Dim 1ch → 512, Dim 2ch → 511, CW/WW 8-bit → 511, CW/WW 16-bit → 509, CW/WW FX → 505, RGB 8-bit → 510, RGB 16-bit → 507, RGB FX → 503, RGBW 8-bit → 509, RGBW 16-bit → 505, RGBW FX → 501, RGBWA 8-bit → 508, RGBWA 16-bit → 503, RGBWA FX → 499. Changing a port to a larger personality re-clamps its start down if it would overhang. Offset apply mode spaces ports by the widest footprint (14ch → 56ch across a 4-port, 112ch across an 8-port).

Notes: Dim Snap, Dim Fade and Dim Random move only the level: the desk's colour channels stay the colour source (Dim Snap snaps one port on per beat in turn across the unit; Dim Fade runs the same chase smoothly, each port swelling up to its beat and down as the next rises; Dim Random pulses each port softly at random). From firmware 3.3.0 the bands are 10 wide and follow the list order, the three Dim effects first (before, they were 11 wide in a different order, and Dim Snap was called Dim Chase). Siren and Police alternate phase across ports (lightbar feel); Dim Snap, Dim Fade, Club, Party, Pulse, Strobe and Police run on the rig's beat clock (128 BPM by default; the app's tap tempo puts the downbeat on the music). Party hops hue every 4th beat and renders greens as teal — never black, never strobes; Club keeps to deep blue through crimson; Traffic plays passing-headlight sweeps with independent random timing per port; Water, Pulse and Clouds never go fully dark; effect speed (CH9) does not affect the strobe rate. The film gags: Fluoro is a failing fluorescent tube (rapid failed strikes, then it catches and holds with a faint mains buzz); Paparazzi fires irregular hard white pops, independent per port, like a press pit; Explosion is a white-hot flash blooming to an orange fireball with an ember-flicker decay; Clouds drifts daylight slowly dimmer and cooler as cloud passes the sun, then back.

Rig-wide FX controls (config, not DMX): FX Tempo (60–240 BPM, tap-settable from the app or set in panel Setup) sets the beat for the beat effects and the speed of the rest, and FX Spread (0–100%) staggers the effect clock across ports. Defaults (128 BPM, 0%) reproduce the behaviour above.

Sources

The rig accepts wired DMX (5-pin) and CRMX wireless (LumenRadio). One source owns the decode at a time, selected in the Sources hub (panel or app).

Hold-on-loss: if the active source drops, every port holds its last look — nothing ever auto-runs on signal loss; changes made after the loss apply immediately. Runt packets (under 25 slots) are rejected as line noise rather than treated as a new look.

RDM

Root device (sub 0): rack footprint, start address = port 1; SET fans out per the Apply mode (JMB parameter 0x8001: 1=Same 2=Seq 3=Offset). Each port is a sub-device (1–N) with its own start address, personality and identify. E1.37-1 CURVE (dimmer curve) and MODULATION_FREQUENCY (PWM library: 1=Smooth 8 kHz, 2=Silent 20 kHz, 3=Camera 15.6 kHz, 4–6=Custom 1–3) are supported on the root and on each port. On the root a read returns port 1 and a write goes to every port.

JMB parameter ID On Value
White balance red 0x8010 root, ports 0–255, 255 = full (also warm on a three-output module)
White balance green 0x8011 root, ports 0–255 (also cool on a three-output module)
White balance blue 0x8012 root, ports 0–255
White balance preview 0x8013 root, ports 0 / 1 — acts only with no DMX present; off after 5 min
White balance white 0x8014 root, ports 0–255 — output 4 of a five-output module (cool on CW/WW)
White balance amber 0x8015 root, ports 0–255 — output 5 (warm on CW/WW)
Custom 1 / 2 / 3 carrier 0x8020 / 0x8022 / 0x8024 root Hz, 2000–25000; 0 empties the entry. A port on a custom entry keeps its own CURVE

A value outside its range is refused, never altered. The root also exposes each port's temperature and output current as E1.20 sensors, and personality is settable at the root for sub-0-only consoles.
