JMB Lighting — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

JMB Lighting (“the app”) is a companion controller for JMB Lighting LED lighting hardware. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the app collects nothing, has no servers, and sends nothing anywhere by itself. The one thing that can leave your device is a support report, and only if you choose to send it (see below).

What the app does

The app talks directly to your own JMB Lighting controller over Bluetooth Low Energy. It lets you set DMX addresses, colours, effects, scenes and read back live telemetry from the hardware. All of this happens locally between your phone and your controller.

Data we collect

None. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services. The app has no back-end server and never transmits your data over the internet.

Data stored on your device

Settings you create in the app — such as saved scenes, port labels and a show file you export — are stored locally on your device only (in the browser/app storage). You can clear them at any time by clearing the app’s data or deleting the app. Exported show files are saved wherever you choose and are never uploaded.

Support reports

Setup → Support lets you write to us about a problem. It builds a status report on your device and shows you exactly what is in it before anything happens. Nothing is sent unless you send it: the report leaves your device only as an email from your own mail app, or as a file you share yourself, addressed to support@jmblighting.co.uk.

The report contains: the app version; your browser and screen details; the controller’s firmware version, status counters and settings; recent app errors, connection events and the last commands the app sent; and the message you typed.

The report never contains your controller PIN, Wi-Fi name or password, network address or location. Controller names, the controller ID and port labels are left out unless you tick the box to include them.

To find faults that end a session, the app keeps its last few error messages in the app’s local storage on your device. They are never transmitted except inside a report you send.

We use a report only to answer you and to fix the fault, and keep it no longer than that needs. Ask at the address above and we will delete it.

Bluetooth permission

The app requests Bluetooth (and, on some Android versions, nearby-devices/location permission that Android ties to Bluetooth scanning) solely to discover and communicate with your JMB Lighting controller. Location is never read, stored, or used for any other purpose, and nothing is sent off the device.

Children

The app is a professional lighting tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
