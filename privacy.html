JMB Zone — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

JMB Zone (“the app”) is a companion controller for JMB Zone LED lighting hardware. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. In short: the app collects nothing, has no servers, and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app does

The app talks directly to your own JMB Zone controller over Bluetooth Low Energy. It lets you set DMX addresses, colours, effects, scenes and read back live telemetry from the hardware. All of this happens locally between your phone and your controller.

Data we collect

None. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services. The app has no back-end server and never transmits your data over the internet.

Data stored on your device

Settings you create in the app — such as saved scenes, port labels and a show file you export — are stored locally on your device only (in the browser/app storage). You can clear them at any time by clearing the app’s data or deleting the app. Exported show files are saved wherever you choose and are never uploaded.

Bluetooth permission

The app requests Bluetooth (and, on some Android versions, nearby-devices/location permission that Android ties to Bluetooth scanning) solely to discover and communicate with your JMB Zone controller. Location is never read, stored, or used for any other purpose, and nothing is sent off the device.

Children

The app is a professional lighting tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
